Meet the Team

BCH is served by believers who share a desire to encourage others, support Christian creators, and point people to Jesus Christ.

Founder & Podcast Host

Dill

Dill founded the Back Country Hope Podcast with a mission to share the hope of Jesus Christ and encourage believers in their daily walk with God. As the podcast grew, that mission expanded into Back Country Hope Ministries—a community connecting faith, fellowship, media, gaming, sports, and Christian content creation.

Through biblical conversations, testimonies, guest interviews, live content, and community outreach, Dill helps guide BCH toward its central purpose: glorifying God, strengthening believers, and building unity among Christian creators.
