Meet the Team

BCH is served by believers who share a desire to encourage others, support Christian creators, and point people to Jesus Christ.

Founder & Podcast Host

Dill

Dill founded the Back Country Hope Podcast with a mission to share the hope of Jesus Christ and encourage believers in their daily walk with God. As the podcast grew, that mission expanded into Back Country Hope Ministries—a community connecting faith, fellowship, media, gaming, sports, and Christian content creation.

Through biblical conversations, testimonies, guest interviews, live content, and community outreach, Dill helps guide BCH toward its central purpose: glorifying God, strengthening believers, and building unity among Christian creators.

Pastoral staff

Pastor Ryan

Cowboy Preacher · Spiritual Advisor

Pastor Ryan serves on our pastoral staff and is a passionate teacher of God’s Word with a heart for helping others grow in their faith. Known by many as a true BCH superfan, Pastor Ryan brings both biblical wisdom and genuine enthusiasm to everything he does.

Evangelist Dylan had the privilege of serving alongside Pastor Ryan as a young adult director at a local church and witnessed firsthand his dedication to ministry and discipleship. As an evangelist, Pastor Ryan has a God-given ability to communicate the truth of the Gospel clearly, boldly, and without compromise. He is committed to preaching God’s Word faithfully and encouraging others to live according to biblical truth.

At BCH, Pastor Ryan serves as a spiritual advisor and as a member of both the Evangelism Team and the Podcast Team, helping share the message of Christ through multiple platforms. He is also a faith-based content creator on Twitch, where he streams as @CowboyPreacher and offers encouragement, fellowship, and the Gospel of Jesus Christ. On TikTok, he is known as The Black Sheep Preacher, sharing biblical truth, encouragement, and Gospel-centered content with a growing online community.

Content creator

Mik3y_Smash

Faith first. Family always. Game on.

Hey everyone! I’m Mik3y_Smash—a dad to three amazing girls, a gamer, and, most importantly, a man who is continually growing in his walk with Jesus Christ.

I first met Dill when I started my streaming journey shortly after my divorce. I found him through a mutual Discord server and started watching his streams, while he began watching mine. From there, our friendship blossomed! He has helped me grow both as a believer and as a streamer, and BCH Ministries helped me reach Affiliate on Twitch. Through that experience, I saw firsthand the heart Dill has for supporting and uplifting small Christian content creators. His passion for helping others grow, both in their faith and as creators, is a big part of what makes BCH Ministries so special.

My faith is an important part of who I am. I’m not a perfect man, and I don’t claim to have everything figured out. I’m simply someone who is learning to trust the Lord more each day, grow through His Word, and become the man He has called me to be. My journey with Christ is ongoing, and I believe that no matter where we are in life, God is always working in us, teaching us, and drawing us closer to Him.

So, why do I stream? For me, gaming is about more than winning matches or chasing highlights. It’s about having fun, building friendships, sharing laughs, and creating a community where people can come as they are and feel like they belong. Whether we’re playing Marvel Rivals, Fortnite, or another game, my goal is to create a positive space where we can enjoy ourselves, encourage one another, and make great memories along the way.

My hope is that this community reflects the things I believe in: faith, family, friendship, kindness, and a whole lot of laughter. I want people to know that behind the controller is a real person who loves his family, values his faith, and wants to make a positive difference, even in the smallest moments.

At the end of the day, I’m not just here to play games. I’m here to build connections, share my journey, and hopefully be a light for Christ wherever God leads me.

Faith first. Family always. Game on.

Welcome to my corner of the multiverse. I’m glad you’re here!
