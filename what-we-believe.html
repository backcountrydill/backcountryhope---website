Our foundation

What We Believe

Our ministry is centered on Jesus Christ, grounded in Scripture, committed to the Gospel, and dedicated to unity within the Body of Christ.

Christ-centered ministry

The faith behind the mission.

This summary explains the biblical foundation guiding BCH. It is not intended to replace faithful involvement, discipleship, and accountability in a local church.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the trustworthy authority for faith, doctrine, and Christian living.

One God

We believe in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—holy, loving, sovereign, and worthy of all worship.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, born of a virgin, sinless in life, crucified for our sins, raised bodily from the dead, and coming again.

Salvation

We believe all people are sinners in need of grace and that salvation is God’s gift, received through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ—not earned by human works.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit convicts, regenerates, indwells, guides, comforts, equips, and empowers believers to live faithfully and serve God.

The Church

We believe all who belong to Christ are part of His Body and are called to worship, fellowship, discipleship, service, prayer, and mutual encouragement.

The Great Commission

We believe followers of Jesus are called to make disciples, share the Gospel, serve others, and use their gifts to glorify God.

Christian Unity

We believe believers should pursue truth and love together, showing humility, grace, and unity while remaining faithful to biblical conviction.

Prayer & Hope

We believe God hears prayer and that lasting hope, peace, forgiveness, and new life are found in Jesus Christ.

“Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, through the power of the Holy Ghost.”
Romans 15:13, KJV

How belief shapes BCH

Truth lived out through love and service.

These convictions shape what BCH teaches, the voices it supports, the conversations it hosts, and the kind of community it seeks to build.

  • Christ is the center, not personalities or platforms.
  • Scripture guides ministry decisions and content.
  • The Gospel is shared with truth, grace, and compassion.
  • Creators are encouraged to serve with integrity and humility.
  • Community should strengthen—not replace—the local church.
