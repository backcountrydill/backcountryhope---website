Content Creators

BCH connects Christian podcasters, streamers, gamers, sports voices, and digital creators who want to glorify God and encourage others through their platforms.

Creator community

Grow in skill, faith, and connection.

The BCH creator network is designed to encourage Christian creators, strengthen their message, and create opportunities for genuine collaboration.

PodcastsTwitchYouTubeTikTokKickRumbleGamingSports

Who is this for?

BCH welcomes established creators and people who feel called to begin. A large following is not the measure of faithfulness. The focus is on Christ-centered purpose, integrity, teachability, healthy community, and a desire to serve people well.

  • Christian podcast hosts and interviewers
  • Live streamers and gaming creators
  • Video, short-form, and social media creators
  • Faith-based sports commentators and communities
  • Writers, designers, editors, and behind-the-scenes contributors
  • New creators seeking encouragement and direction
Important:

Participation in BCH does not replace accountability to Scripture, a local church, or wise pastoral leadership.

Shared expectations

Creators should represent Christ well.

The strongest creator community is built on trust, consistency, humility, and mutual support.

1
Christ-centered conduct

Use words, content, and community influence responsibly.

2
Biblical integrity

Handle faith-based claims carefully and remain teachable.

3
Genuine participation

Build relationships rather than only promoting your own content.

4
Respectful collaboration

Communicate clearly, honor commitments, and give proper credit.

5
Community safety

Respect boundaries, privacy, moderation, and platform rules.

6
Servant-minded growth

Pursue excellence without making numbers or attention the goal.
