You are welcome here

A growing ministry built around hope, faith, and unity.

Hi, welcome to Back Country Hope Ministries (BCH)! We are a faith-based ministry focused on sharing the hope found in Jesus Christ through podcasts, live streams, gaming, sports, fellowship, and Christian content creation.

At BCH, our mission is to unite, encourage, and support Christian creators, podcasters, and streamers as they use their platforms to glorify God and share the Gospel. What started as a podcast has grown into a ministry dedicated to building a community where believers can connect, collaborate, grow in faith, and make an impact through digital media.

Whether you are a podcast host, streamer, content creator, gamer, sports enthusiast, or someone who feels called to start creating content, BCH is here to support you. We believe in helping creators grow their audience, strengthen their message, and connect with like-minded believers across platforms such as Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, Kick, Rumble, and more.

Through our podcasts, creator network, community events, collaborations, and ministry outreach, we strive to encourage others, promote Christ-centered content, and build unity within the Body of Christ.

No matter where you are in your journey, you are welcome at BCH as we seek the Lord together, encourage one another, and share the hope that can only be found in Jesus Christ.

Flagship podcast

The Back Country Hope Podcast

The Back Country Hope Podcast is the flagship podcast of BCH Ministries, founded by Dill with a mission to share the hope of Jesus Christ and encourage believers in their daily walk with God. What began as a simple podcast has grown into a ministry dedicated to building community, strengthening faith, and connecting Christian content creators and podcasters.

Through biblical discussions, testimonies, ministry conversations, and special guest interviews, we seek to glorify God and point people to Christ. We are passionate about encouraging believers, reaching those searching for hope, and fostering unity within the Christian creator community.

Our episodes cover a variety of topics, including faith, discipleship, evangelism, Christian living, content creation, ministry, and current issues facing believers today. We also highlight and support Christian podcasters, streamers, and content creators who are using their platforms to advance God’s Kingdom.

Whether you’re a longtime believer, new to the faith, or simply curious about Christianity, we invite you to join us as we have honest conversations, grow together in Christ, and share the hope that can only be found in Jesus.
